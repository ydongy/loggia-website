# v1.0 Baseline

> Issue: None · Requirement: `docs/content.md#pages` · Screens: None · Status: in progress

## Goal

A visitor can learn what Loggia does, download the current release, see the price, read the Terms of Service,
Privacy Policy and Refund Policy, and contact support, in any of nine languages, and the app can link to each of
those pages by a stable address. The site is static, collects nothing and passes its checks in every language.

## Scope

- Home page with the link chain, five screenshots per language, the four lamp states, account facts, three
  steps, the download section and the plans table with the price.
- FAQ, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and changelog pages.
- Nine languages, each page at one address; the first visit to an English page opens the browser's language.
- Download button reading the release feed; official-download notice; footer with the legal links and contact
  address.
- Strict Content-Security-Policy, no cookies, no third-party resources; Cloudflare Web Analytics is the only analytics (0008).
- Five checks in CI (`check_i18n`, `build`, clean `public/`, `check_site`, `check_facts`) and the documentation
  check.

## Non-goals

- Accounts, a customer portal or any server-side code.
- Analytics other than Cloudflare Web Analytics, cookies or consent banners.
- A blog, documentation pages or marketing pages beyond the home page.
- Redirects, aliases and separate download or pricing pages.

## Decisions

- Plain HTML generated by standard-library Python, committed in `public/`:
  `docs/adr/0001-no-framework.md`.
- No cookies, and no analytics other than Cloudflare Web Analytics: `docs/adr/0008-cloudflare-web-analytics.md` (replaces 0002).
- One address per page, no redirects: `docs/adr/0003-one-address-per-page.md`.
- A strict Content-Security-Policy: `docs/adr/0004-content-security-policy.md`.
- The generator is the vendored ydongy sitekit: `docs/adr/0005-vendored-sitekit.md`.
- Real screenshots in a scroll-snap carousel: `docs/adr/0006-screenshot-carousel.md`.
- Cloudflare Workers static assets: `docs/adr/0007-cloudflare-workers-static-assets.md`.
- The facts in the copy come from the app's facts table, mirrored in `docs/content.md` and enforced by
  `scripts/check_facts.py`, so a number cannot differ between pages or languages.

## Tests

- `scripts/sitekit/check_i18n.py` checks every language file against English: structure, HTML tags, links and
  placeholders, no dashes.
- `scripts/sitekit/check_site.py` checks that every language has every page, that links and anchors resolve
  and that nothing violates the Content-Security-Policy.
- `scripts/check_facts.py` checks the Facts table against all nine languages, the page names, the price row,
  the footer links and the absence of redirects.
- By hand in a browser, in light and dark and at phone width: the home page, the legal pages, the language
  menu, the carousel with and without JavaScript, the download button with the feed reachable and not.
