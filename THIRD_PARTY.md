## Notes

The site ships no third-party code, fonts or images. Text is set in the visitor's system fonts, the three
scripts in `public/` are written for this site, and the icon, screenshots and share image are the product's own.
The page generator is ydongy's own `sitekit`, vendored in `scripts/sitekit/`. `wrangler` (Cloudflare) is
run with `npx` to preview and deploy; it is a tool, not part of the repository or the site.
