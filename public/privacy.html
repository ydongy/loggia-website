Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 2026

This policy covers the Loggia app and this website: what is collected, who it is sent to, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

To activate, verify or deactivate Pro, Loggia sends the license key and a device label (your Mac’s name plus a short code derived from a hardware identifier) to Dodo Payments over HTTPS. Nothing else is sent.

Loggia has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own.

2. What we don’t collect

Loggia does not collect or receive the services you publish, your domains, your Cloudflare API token, the traffic that reaches your services, or how you use the app. Everything runs on your Mac or in your own Cloudflare account. Visitors reach your services through Cloudflare, straight to your Mac.

Logs stay on your Mac. Exporting diagnostics creates a file that you send yourself; tokens, license key and your home folder are removed from it.

3. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare: Loggia calls the Cloudflare API with your token to manage tunnels, domain records and access protection in your account. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy applies to your account.
  • GitHub: to download cloudflared and to check for a newer version of it.
  • dl.useloggia.com: to check for a new version of Loggia, once a day unless you turn off automatic checks. The request carries the app name and version, the update framework’s version and your IP address; no system profile is sent.
  • Public DNS: to check that your domains resolve.
  • Dodo Payments: our payment provider and the merchant of record. At checkout Dodo collects your name, email address and billing details under its own privacy policy, and handles invoices, taxes and refunds. Loggia does not receive your payment details.
  • This website: static, with no cookies. It and the downloads are served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard access logs. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures page views and page load times. According to Cloudflare, it uses no cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint visitors and does not collect personal data. The app does not use it.

4. Data storage

  • Your Cloudflare API token and your Pro license key are kept in your Mac’s keychain.
  • Settings and the service list are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Loggia; the connector’s log is in ~/Library/Logs/Loggia.
  • The developer can see your purchase record (name, email address, payment ID) in the Dodo dashboard, only to give support and process refunds.

5. Your rights and control

You can deactivate your license in Settings › License at any time. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email support@useloggia.com; we reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EU or the EEA, you also have the rights the GDPR gives you, including data portability and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.

Questions about Loggia, a purchase or a refund? Email support@useloggia.com.
