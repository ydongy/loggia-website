Only your own account.

No servers in between

Loggia runs on your Cloudflare account, with no third-party servers in between.

Your token stays on your Mac

The API token is kept in your Mac’s keychain and is only sent to Cloudflare.

No analytics in the app, no sign-up

Settings and the service list stay on your Mac. Only activating Pro sends anything about you: a license key and a device label, to Dodo Payments. See the Privacy Policy.

Only the people you list

Turn on access protection for a service and visitors sign in with a one-time code sent to an email you allowed.

Set up in three steps.

  1. Paste a Cloudflare API token

    “Create a Token on Cloudflare” opens a template with the permissions already selected.

  2. Let Loggia prepare the runtime

    It downloads Cloudflare’s cloudflared and keeps it running for you.

  3. Add a service and publish it

    Pick a local port, then turn on Publish to Internet and choose a domain.
