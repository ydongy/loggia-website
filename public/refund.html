Refund Policy

Last updated · October 2026

1. 14-day refund

If Loggia Pro does not work for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying it. You do not need to give a reason.

2. How to ask

Email support@useloggia.com from the address you bought with, and include the payment ID from your receipt if you have it. Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments to the original payment method; banks usually take 3 to 5 business days to show it.

3. What happens to your license

Once a refund is done, the license key stops working and Loggia goes back to the free plan. Nothing is taken offline: services you already published keep running. How the free plan treats them is explained in the FAQ. You can buy Pro again at any time.

4. Cancellation

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect rights that the law gives you as a consumer.

Questions about Loggia, a purchase or a refund? Email support@useloggia.com.
