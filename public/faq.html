Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? The app itself tells you what is wrong and which button fixes it. Open the service or link details first.

Basics

Is Loggia free?

The free plan is. It can publish up to 2 services to the internet and can’t turn on new access protection. Pro removes these limits, as a one-time purchase. See Pricing.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 26 or later, a Cloudflare account, and at least one domain managed by Cloudflare.

Do I have to pay Cloudflare?

Publishing uses Cloudflare Tunnel, which is free. Access protection needs Zero Trust set up on your account, and the free plan is enough. Your domain is billed by its registrar as usual.

Does my Mac have to stay on?

Yes. Your services keep running on your Mac; Loggia only gives them a public address. While the Mac is asleep, off or offline, the address can’t be reached. The connector starts at login, so services come back as soon as your Mac does.

Does my traffic go through your servers?

No. Visitors reach your services through Cloudflare, straight to your Mac. Loggia has no servers of its own and does not see your traffic. See the Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare and the API token

Which permissions does the API token need?

In onboarding or in Settings › Cloudflare, click Create a Token on Cloudflare. It opens a template with the permissions already selected, so you only need to confirm it. The template includes:

Account › Cloudflare Tunnel › EditRequired
Zone › DNS › EditRequired. Zone Resources can be limited to the domains you use.
Account › Access: Apps and Policies › EditOptional, for access protection
Account › Access: Organizations, Identity Providers, and Groups › EditOptional, lets Loggia set up the email-code login for you

Settings › Cloudflare lists each permission and has Copy Permission List if you prefer to create the token yourself. The token is stored only in your Mac’s keychain.

I just fixed the token’s permissions, but Loggia still says they’re missing.

Changes on Cloudflare take a minute or two to take effect. Wait a moment, then click Check Again in Settings › Cloudflare.

Why can’t I turn on access protection?
  • Access protection is a Pro feature.
  • The service has to be published first.
  • The token needs both Access permissions listed above.
  • Your Cloudflare account needs Zero Trust set up. The free plan is enough. Once it is set up, click Check Again in Settings › Cloudflare.
With access protection on, who can open my service?

Only the email addresses you list, or everyone at a domain if you add @example.com. Visitors get a one-time code by email. A login lasts 24 hours by default, and you can change that.

With Pro you can also add trusted networks, such as your home or office internet address. Visitors from a trusted network open the service without the code, and so does anyone else on the same Wi-Fi or mobile network.

Will Loggia overwrite a domain I already use?

Not silently. If the domain already points somewhere else, Loggia stops and asks you before changing anything.

Pro and licenses

How many Macs can use one license?

Two. To use it on a different Mac, first click Deactivate in Settings › License on one of the Macs that has it.

Does Pro need an internet connection?

Activating needs one. After that Loggia checks the license once a day. If it can’t reach the license server, Pro keeps working for 30 days after the last successful check, and comes back on its own once the Mac is online again.

Which versions does a license cover?

All 1.x versions. Updates themselves are free on both plans.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of buying. The Refund Policy says how to ask and what happens to your license.

What happens after a refund, or if a license stops being valid?

Loggia goes back to the free plan, and nothing is taken offline: services you published keep running. The free plan manages only the first 2 published services in your list. The others keep running, but Loggia stops checking them and shows them as Not managed; you can still stop publishing them. Access protection that is already on keeps working; you can turn it off but not change it. Upgrading to Pro again restores everything.

Troubleshooting

A service shows as down. Where do I start?

Open the service. Loggia shows which part of the link is broken (your local service, the domain record, the connector on your Mac, or Cloudflare), why, and the button that fixes it. A new domain record can take a few minutes to take effect.

Loggia says a proxy app is intercepting the connection.

In enhanced or TUN mode with fake IP, a proxy app answers for argotunnel.com with fake addresses, so cloudflared keeps at most two connections to Cloudflare, or none. When cloudflared can’t reach Cloudflare, Loggia checks for this and only then names a proxy app. In the proxy app:

  1. Route argotunnel.com and cftunnel.com directly.
  2. Add +.argotunnel.com to the fake-IP filter (fake-ip-filter), so it resolves to Cloudflare’s real addresses. In fake-IP mode a direct rule alone is not enough.
  3. Click Reconnect, Try Again or Restart, whichever Loggia shows.

The status of your public services is checked from the internet and is not affected by the proxy app. If a domain you just published opens elsewhere but not on this Mac, the proxy app may remember it as missing for up to half an hour: add the domain to the fake-IP filter too, or clear the proxy app’s DNS cache.

Where can I see what the connector is doing?

Settings › Connector shows whether cloudflared is installed and running, its version and last exit code, and its log. If something keeps failing, you can export diagnostics there.

Updates and uninstalling

How do I update Loggia?

Loggia checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, it shows what changed and can download it, install it and relaunch for you. To check right away, click Check for Updates in Settings › About; to stop the daily check, turn off Check for updates automatically there. Your settings and services are kept, and updates are free on both plans. The changelog lists what changed in each version.

How do I uninstall Loggia completely?
  1. In Settings › Cloudflare, click Remove Everything… to delete Loggia’s domain records, routes and access protection from Cloudflare.
  2. Quit Loggia and move it from Applications to the Trash.
  3. In Terminal, run launchctl bootout gui/$(id -u)/com.useloggia.app.cloudflared, then delete ~/Library/Application Support/Loggia, ~/Library/Logs/Loggia and ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.useloggia.app.cloudflared.plist.
  4. Delete the “Loggia” items in Keychain Access.
